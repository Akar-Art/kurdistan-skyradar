Four regions of Kurdistan

The atlas brings together Bakur, Başûr, Rojava and Rojhilat, the northern, southern, western and eastern regions of Kurdistan. Kurdish place names connect the map with the histories and cultures of its cities and villages. Historical boundaries, modern divisions and cultural areas are separate map layers: they describe different subjects and periods.

Maps, cities and landscapes

Explore city histories and galleries, built heritage, wildlife profiles, rivers and mountains. The map gallery opens curated views of borders, dialects, religious communities and relief. City profiles connect stories with nearby landmarks and offer a View on map action to return to their geographic setting.

Sources and contributions

The atlas combines geographic datasets with curated cultural and historical records. Photo credits identify their rights holders and licenses. Credits and sources are listed in the updates log, alongside dated additions and corrections. To suggest a correction or a missing place, close this panel and use Contribute or Add your village on the map.
